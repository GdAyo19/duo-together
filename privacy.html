A clear, simple guide

Privacy, in plain language.

Tandem stores practice on your device in preview mode. If you connect an account and shared space, it uses Supabase to sign you in and sync shared activity.

When you use preview mode

Your language choice, practice, and saved phrases are kept in this browser’s local storage. They stay on this device and are not shared across browsers or devices. Clearing this browser’s site data can remove them.

When cloud sync is connected

Sign-in and account details are handled by the Supabase project configured for Tandem. A shared space can store its name, language, member display names, practice records, and saved phrases. Database policies limit shared records to members of that space; Supabase processes and hosts this data.

If you use Google sign-in, Google also handles its part of the authentication flow. Tandem does not receive your Google password.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not include an analytics or advertising integration. The site does load fonts from Google Fonts; your browser requests those font files from Google when the font is not already available locally.

Your choices

  • Use preview mode without signing in; its practice stays in this browser.
  • Sign out from your account controls to end the current session on this device.
  • To manage or remove cloud account data, use the Supabase project that operates this deployment.

Tandem is an independent project. For service-specific account and data handling, review the privacy information from Supabase or Google as applicable.
